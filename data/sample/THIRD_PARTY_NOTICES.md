# Third-party data notices

The sample arrays contain limited processed values derived from the products
listed in `configs/data_sources.json`, including GDHY yield, ERA5-Land weather,
PKU GIMMS NDVI, GLASS rEC-LUE GPP, and crop-calendar/support information.

The sample is provided only to document the PhenoBIID software interface and
to enable small-scale inference checks. No ownership of an upstream product is
claimed, no upstream license is replaced, and permission to redistribute a
complete source product is not granted. Users remain responsible for obtaining
full products from their official records and complying with the corresponding
terms and citation requirements.
